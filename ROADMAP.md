# Praxis roadmap

Praxis recommends three projects from personal catalog evidence and develops a
selected project brief. Scope and constraints are in [architecture](docs/architecture.md);
working conventions are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

Publication and metered model checks require explicit approval. Local tests alone
do not establish deployed correctness or recommendation quality.

## Remaining verification

- [ ] Run infrastructure formatting, validation, and static checks in hosted CI.
- [ ] Verify dashboard console rendering.
- [ ] Confirm operational email subscription and notification delivery.
- [ ] Verify a related failure-and-recovery trace through the application.
- [ ] Verify complete fresh-environment provisioning.
- [ ] Publish the repository after explicit authorization to change its visibility.

Keep deployment selections, scan reports, approvals, and operational handoff notes
in ignored local files, not this roadmap.
